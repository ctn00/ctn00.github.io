Bestie Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Who uses this app

Bestie is a personal assistant used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What Google data it accesses

  • YouTube: the owner's channel analytics, video details and comments, and, only when the owner approves each change, video titles, descriptions and playlists.
  • Gmail: reading the owner's email to surface deadlines and important messages.
  • Google Calendar: reading the owner's events to build a daily briefing.
  • Google Drive: searching the owner's files on request.

How the data is used and stored

Data is processed on a server the owner runs on their own computer. Relevant excerpts may be sent to an AI model provider to generate summaries and replies for the owner. Notes the assistant writes are stored privately on the owner's own computer. Data is never sold, shared for advertising, or used for any purpose other than assisting the owner.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Bestie's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
